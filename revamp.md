### The strategic idea

**FluxFuse should be the umbrella.**

Don't force yourself to decide today whether FluxFuse is an AI automation company, SaaS company, software company, design company, etc.

It's the **company that builds and delivers technology products and solutions.**

That gives you enormous room to monetize.

Your automation business can grow independently. Your browser extensions can grow independently. Your SaaS products can grow independently. Your desktop software can grow independently. And if one of them becomes huge, **FluxFuse remains the company behind it.**

And importantly, this fits much better with what you're actually trying to do: **build multiple monetizable products while keeping one credible corporate identity rather than reinventing yourself for every product.**
