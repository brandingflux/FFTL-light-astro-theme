### Section 3: How It Works: 3 Steps to One-Take Confidence

#### Step 1: Open Your Script Right Under Your Webcam
Click the extension on any tab. Your script appears inside a sleek, dark notch floating directly below your computer camera. Your eyes stay aligned with the lens naturally.

#### Step 2: Just Talk. FLUXPROMPTER Follows Your Voice.
No rushing. No lagging. When you speak, the text moves. When you pause to take a sip of water or emphasize a key point, the prompter stops and waits for you. Speak fast, speak slow, or ad-lib an unscripted joke: FLUXPROMPTER stays locked with your voice.

#### Step 3: Finish Calmly and See Your Delivery Score
When you say your final word, the prompter gently concludes your take and reveals your presentation score: your speaking pace (WPM), composure rating, and verbal filler crutches ("um", "like", "you know").

### Section 4: Features You Will Fall in Love With

#### 1. The Camera Notch: Unbroken Eye Contact
Most teleprompters span the entire width of your screen, forcing your eyes to bounce back and forth like a tennis match. FLUXPROMPTER holds your script in a narrow 440-pixel column anchored directly below your camera. Your audience will swear you memorized every single word.

#### 2. Smart Voice Follow: Never Get Rushed Again
Traditional prompters move at a mechanical speed that forces you to sprint or drag. FLUXPROMPTER listens through your microphone and scrolls dynamically as you speak:
- Talk fast during an exciting point? The prompter speeds up.
- Pause for dramatic effect? The prompter freezes and waits for you.
- International accent? FLUXPROMPTER understands your natural pronunciation without any configuration.

#### 3. Pocket Remote: Turn Your Phone into a Secret Controller
Do not reach for your keyboard or lean into the camera. Scan a quick QR code with your phone camera, and your phone transforms into a silent, wireless controller. Keep your phone in your lap or in your pocket and tap to pause, resume, or adjust scroll speed without moving your hands on screen.

#### 4. Visual Breathing Badges: Never Run Out of Air
When speaking under pressure, it is easy to blow through periods and forget to breathe. FLUXPROMPTER automatically highlights punctuation in warm colors:
- Amber dots cue full diaphragm breaths.
- Emerald markers guide vocal inflection lifts.
- Stage directions like `[SMILE]` or `[PAUSE 2s]` remind you to connect warmly with your audience.

#### 5. Meeting Stealth Mode: Completely Invisible on Screen Shares
Sharing your screen on Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams? Press `Alt + S`. The teleprompter turns semi-transparent, and all mouse clicks pass straight through to your slide deck or web browser. You can click buttons, change slides, and navigate software while your script remains clearly visible right in front of your eyes.

#### 6. Post-Take Debrief: Your Personal Speaking Coach
After every session, FLUXPROMPTER gives you instant, private coaching:
- **Composure Score**: See how steadily and calmly you delivered your script.
- **Pacing Check**: Discover whether you spoke at an optimal conversational rhythm (130-150 WPM) or rushed through nervous sections.
- **Filler Word Radar**: Identify exactly where you said "um", "uh", or "actually" so you can eliminate them on your next recording.

### Section 8: Frequently Asked Questions

#### Will people on my Zoom or Teams call be able to see the teleprompter?
No. FLUXPROMPTER runs locally as an overlay in your browser. When you share a specific application window or presentation tab, or when you are on camera, other meeting attendees only see you and your presentation. The floating notch stays strictly on your screen.

#### Do I need to buy any extra hardware, pedals, or special glasses?
No extra hardware is needed. FLUXPROMPTER works with your existing laptop screen, built-in webcam, or external camera. If you do own professional teleprompter glass, FLUXPROMPTER includes built-in horizontal and vertical mirror modes.

#### What if I have a regional accent or English is my second language?
FLUXPROMPTER was built specifically with universal accent recognition. Whether you speak with an Indian, Arabic, Asian, European, or regional American accent, the voice-matching engine adapts to your speech automatically without any configuration.

#### Is my audio private? Does this upload my voice to the cloud?
Your privacy is completely protected. FLUXPROMPTER processes 100% of your voice locally on your computer using built-in browser speech engines. Zero audio recordings, voice clips, or script files are ever sent to external cloud servers or third parties.

#### How much does it cost?
FLUXPROMPTER is completely free to install and use. No credit card is required, and there are no hidden paywalls on core prompter features.
